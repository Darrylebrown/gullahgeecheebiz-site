Gullah Banana Pudding Recipe

Published July 28, 2026 · Gullah Geechee Biz

Layers of vanilla wafers, fresh bananas, and creamy pudding topped with meringue. This Gullah banana pudding recipe is the ultimate Southern dessert.

🍳
⏱️
Prep
20m
🔥
Cook
15m
⏲️
Total
35m
🍽️
Servings
10

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 box vanilla wafers (about 12 oz)
  • 4 ripe bananas, sliced
  • 4 large egg whites
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
  • 6 tbsp sugar (for meringue)

Instructions

  1. In a medium saucepan, whisk together sugar, cornstarch, and salt. Gradually whisk in milk.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thickened and bubbly, about 8 minutes.
  3. In a small bowl, beat egg yolks. Gradually whisk 1 cup of hot milk mixture into yolks, then pour back into saucepan.
  4. Cook 2 more minutes, stirring constantly. Remove from heat. Stir in vanilla and butter.
  5. Layer vanilla wafers, sliced bananas, and pudding in a 9x13 dish. Repeat layers, ending with pudding on top.
  6. Preheat oven to 350°F. Beat egg whites and cream of tartar until soft peaks form. Gradually add 6 tbsp sugar, beating until stiff peaks.
  7. Spread meringue over pudding, sealing to edges. Bake 10-12 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Cool completely, then refrigerate at least 4 hours before serving.
💡 Pro Tip: The meringue topping is what makes this Gullah banana pudding special — don't skip it. Make it a day ahead for the best flavor as the wafers soften into cake-like layers.
