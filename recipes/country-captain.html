Gullah Country Captain Chicken Recipe

Published July 28, 2026 · Gullah Geechee Biz

A curried chicken stew with tomatoes, peppers, and currants — served over rice. This Gullah country captain recipe has roots in the spice trade through Charleston port.

🍳
⏱️
Prep
15m
🔥
Cook
45m
⏲️
Total
1m
🍽️
Servings
6

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs chicken pieces (thighs and legs)
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1/4 cup currants or raisins
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup sliced almonds, toasted
  • Cooked rice for serving
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
  2. Brown chicken in batches, about 4 minutes per side. Remove and set aside.
  3. Add onion and bell pepper to the pot. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 minute.
  4. Stir in curry powder and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  5. Add diced tomatoes, chicken broth, currants, vinegar, thyme, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
  6. Return chicken to the pot, nestling into the sauce. Bring to a boil.
  7. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer 30-35 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
  8. Serve over rice. Garnish with toasted almonds and parsley.
💡 Pro Tip: Country Captain is a classic Charleston dish that dates back to the 19th century spice trade. The curry powder and currants reflect the global influences that came through the port.
