Gullah Smothered Pork Chops Recipe

Published July 28, 2026 · Gullah Geechee Biz

Thick pork chops smothered in a rich onion gravy. This Gullah smothered pork chops recipe is the ultimate Lowcountry comfort food.

🍳
⏱️
Prep
15m
🔥
Cook
45m
⏲️
Total
1m
🍽️
Servings
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in pork chops (1-inch thick)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Season pork chops with salt, pepper, and smoked paprika. Dredge in flour, shaking off excess. Reserve remaining flour.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown pork chops 3-4 minutes per side. Remove and set aside.
  3. Add sliced onions to the skillet. Cook until deeply caramelized, about 10 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 minute.
  4. Sprinkle 2 tbsp of the reserved flour over onions. Stir and cook 2 minutes.
  5. Slowly whisk in chicken broth, thyme, cayenne, and bay leaves. Bring to a simmer.
  6. Return pork chops to the skillet, nestling into the gravy. Cover and reduce heat to low.
  7. Simmer 25-30 minutes until pork is tender and cooked through.
  8. Stir in vinegar. Remove bay leaves. Garnish with parsley. Serve with rice or mashed potatoes.
💡 Pro Tip: The key to smothered pork chops is patience — let the onions caramelize deeply and the meat simmer low and slow. Serve with rice to soak up the gravy.
