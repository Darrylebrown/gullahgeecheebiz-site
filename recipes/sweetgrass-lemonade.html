Gullah Sweetgrass Lemonade Recipe

Published July 28, 2026 · Gullah Geechee Biz

Refreshing lemonade with a hint of sweetgrass and mint. This Gullah sweetgrass lemonade recipe is the taste of a Lowcountry summer afternoon.

🍳
⏱️
Prep
10m
🔥
Cook
10m
⏲️
Total
20m
🍽️
Servings
8

Ingredients

  • 6 cups water
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup fresh lemon juice (about 6-8 lemons)
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, packed
  • 2 tbsp dried sweetgrass or 1/4 cup fresh sweetgrass (if available)
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced for garnish
  • Ice for serving
  • Mint sprigs for garnish

Instructions

  1. In a small saucepan, combine 2 cups water and sugar. Bring to a boil, stirring until sugar dissolves. Remove from heat.
  2. Add mint leaves and sweetgrass to the simple syrup. Let steep for 10 minutes.
  3. Strain the syrup through a fine-mesh strainer into a pitcher. Discard solids.
  4. Add remaining 4 cups water and fresh lemon juice. Stir to combine.
  5. Refrigerate until cold, at least 2 hours.
  6. Serve over ice. Garnish with lemon slices and mint sprigs.
💡 Pro Tip: Sweetgrass (Hierochloe odorata) is the same grass used in traditional Gullah sweetgrass basket weaving. It has a sweet, vanilla-like aroma. If unavailable, the mint alone makes a delicious lemonade.
