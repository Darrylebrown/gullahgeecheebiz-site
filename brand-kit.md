# Gullah Geechee Biz — Brand Kit

## Official Design System (Adopted July 26, 2026)

### Brand Essence
Ralph Lauren meets National Geographic meets African diaspora luxury. Timeless, warm, sophisticated, rooted in the Lowcountry.

### Brand Soul (Non-Negotiable)
The luxury is the frame. The culture is the picture. Every output must feel like it comes FROM the Gullah Geechee community, not like it's observing it from outside.

**What keeps us relatable:**
- The people come first — not the brand
- The marsh, the water, the wildlife are characters, not backdrops
- The language is warm and human, not corporate
- The photography feels like a family album, not a stock photo
- The stories are told by us, not about us
- Every design element has a cultural reason — sweetgrass weave patterns, indigo dye traditions, basket coil motifs
- The gold isn't flashy — it's the color of golden hour over the marsh, the color of a setting sun on the water
- The navy isn't corporate — it's the night sky over St. Helena Island, the deep water of a tidal creek
