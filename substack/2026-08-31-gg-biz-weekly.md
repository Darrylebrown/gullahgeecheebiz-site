**Subject: The Secret History They Don't Teach You in School**

Dear Friends,

Did you know that the Gullah Geechee people are recognized by UNESCO as having preserved West African culture more completely than any other diaspora community in the Americas?

For over 400 years, the Gullah Geechee people of the Sea Islands from North Carolina to Florida have maintained:

• Their language (Gullah) — a creole language with strong West African roots
• Traditional basket weaving techniques passed down through generations
• Spiritual practices like the Ring Shout, one of the oldest Black American dance forms
• Culinary traditions like red rice, hoppin' John, and shrimp and grits
• Deep knowledge of the coastal environment and tidal systems

This isn't just history — it's living culture. And it's under threat from development, climate change, and cultural erosion.
