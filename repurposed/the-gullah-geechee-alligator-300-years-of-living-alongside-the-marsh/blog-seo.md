# The Gullah Geechee Alligator — 300 Years of Living Alongside the Marsh

The American alligator has been swimming in Lowcountry waters for thousands of years. But for the Gullah Geechee people, the alligator is more than just an animal. It's a symbol of survival. Gullah Geechee ancestors learned to read the alligator's movements — where it basks, where it hunts, where it nests. These patterns told them about the health of the marsh, the quality of the water, and the best times to fish. Today, the alligator is one of the most iconic animals of the Gullah Geechee Corridor. A video of an alligator in a Lowcountry marsh recently went viral with over half a million views. People are fascinated by these ancient creatures. But the real story is the people who have lived alongside them for 300 years.
