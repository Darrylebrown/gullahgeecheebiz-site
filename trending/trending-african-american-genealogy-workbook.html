Why this matters to Gullah Geechee culture:

The Gullah Geechee people have one of the most documented yet fragmented genealogies in America. Tracing family lines through slavery, Reconstruction, and the present day is both a challenge and a sacred duty. Every tool that helps reconnect families is preserving history.
