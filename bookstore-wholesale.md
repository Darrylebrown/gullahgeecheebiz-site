## Sell Sheet — One Pager

A one-page PDF with:
- Gullah Geechee Biz logo (navy + gold)
- "Authentic Gullah Geechee Books — Direct from the Lowcountry"
- List of available titles with cover images
- Wholesale pricing table
- Ordering info: email, website, turnaround time
- "55% off retail — highest margins in the industry"
- Print-on-demand — no minimums, no returns

## How It Works

1. Store emails an order (title + quantity)
2. We process through print-on-demand (IngramSpark or similar)
3. Books ship direct to store within 5-7 business days
4. Store pays wholesale price on net-30 terms
5. No minimum order — even 1 book works
