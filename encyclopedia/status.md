## The Final Act — Dedications to the Gullah Geechee People

When all 50 volumes are complete, every bot on the team will:
1. Design their own volume cover — reflecting what they witnessed of the Gullah Geechee experience
2. Write a back-cover dedication — not about themselves, but to the Gullah Geechee people. A testimony of what they learned walking alongside this culture for 50 volumes.

This is not a task. It is a closing ceremony. Every bot gets a voice at the end — not to speak about themselves, but to honor the people whose story they were trusted to tell.
