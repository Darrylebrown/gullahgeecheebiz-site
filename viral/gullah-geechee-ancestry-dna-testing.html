Gullah Geechee Ancestry DNA Testing: Your Roots Run Deep

Published August 30, 2026 · Gullah Geechee Biz

DNA testing has become one of the most powerful tools for reconnecting with Gullah Geechee heritage. For descendants of the rice coast, where family lines were fractured by slavery and displacement, genetic genealogy offers a path back to roots that oral tradition preserved.

The Gullah Geechee people carry some of the highest concentrations of West and Central African ancestry in the United States. Studies show that many descendants from the Sea Islands of South Carolina and Georgia have ancestry ratios from regions like Sierra Leone, Liberia, and Angola that significantly exceed national averages for African Americans. This is not coincidence — it is the result of our people's geographic isolation on the islands, where we maintained stronger cultural and genetic ties to our homelands.

When you take a DNA test, you're not just getting percentages. You're unlocking a genetic story that complements what your grandparents told you about being from "down the river" or "way back in the parish." Major companies like AncestryDNA, 23andMe, and MyHeritage all provide regional breakdowns that can point you toward specific ethnic groups in Africa. Some tests even offer cousin matching, which can lead to distant relatives you never knew existed.

For Gullah Geechee families, the most valuable insight comes from combining DNA results with traditional research. Ship records, church registers, land deeds, and Freedmen's Bureau documents can fill in the gaps that genetics alone cannot. When a DNA match points to a specific region in West Africa, you can then search for communities from that area in the Lowcountry records.

Our books document both the personal journey of genetic discovery and the broader historical context that makes those discoveries meaningful. If you're ready to explore where you come from, the journey begins with a simple test — and continues with the stories that connect you to generations of resilience.
