Why This Collection Matters

The Gullah Geechee culture has survived for over 300 years, preserving African roots in language, craft, food, and faith on the Sea Islands. But much of that heritage has gone unrecorded in mainstream books. This encyclopedia was created to ensure the story is told completely and accurately — by and for the community and the scholars and descendants who carry it forward.
