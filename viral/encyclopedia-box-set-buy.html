Why This Matters

The Gullah Geechee people are one of the most culturally significant communities in America. Our language, food, music, and traditions trace directly to West Africa — preserved for over 300 years despite slavery, Jim Crow, and relentless development pressure.

This encyclopedia is not just a book. It is a living archive. A record that cannot be erased. A gift to every family that wants to pass down their heritage to the next generation.

About the Author

Darryl Elliott Brown is a Gullah Geechee writer, historian, and cultural preservationist from Beaufort, South Carolina. He is the founder of Gullah Geechee Biz, a digital media company dedicated to documenting and sharing Gullah Geechee culture through books, podcasts, documentaries, and digital archives.
