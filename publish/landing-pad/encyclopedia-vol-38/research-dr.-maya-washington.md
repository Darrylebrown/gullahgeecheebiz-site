## Research Topic: Reconstruction era in the Sea Islands

### Key Findings

1. **Historical Context**: The Gullah Geechee people have preserved African traditions in the Sea Islands for over 400 years. Reconstruction era in the Sea Islands represents a vital thread in this cultural tapestry.

2. **Cultural Significance**: This topic is central to understanding Gullah Geechee identity and continuity. Community knowledge holders emphasize its importance across generations.

3. **Contemporary Relevance**: Modern scholarship and community efforts continue to explore and preserve reconstruction era in the sea islands, ensuring it remains a living tradition.
