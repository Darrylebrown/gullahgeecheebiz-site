## Research Topic: Sea Islands geography and ecology

### Key Findings

1. **Historical Context**: The Gullah Geechee people have preserved African traditions in the Sea Islands for over 400 years. Sea Islands geography and ecology represents a vital thread in this cultural tapestry.

2. **Cultural Significance**: This topic is central to understanding Gullah Geechee identity and continuity. Community knowledge holders emphasize its importance across generations.

3. **Contemporary Relevance**: Modern scholarship and community efforts continue to explore and preserve sea islands geography and ecology, ensuring it remains a living tradition.
