## Research Topic: The Middle Passage and Gullah Geechee ancestry

### Key Findings

1. **Historical Context**: The Gullah Geechee people have preserved African traditions in the Sea Islands for over 400 years. The Middle Passage and Gullah Geechee ancestry represents a vital thread in this cultural tapestry.

2. **Cultural Significance**: This topic is central to understanding Gullah Geechee identity and continuity. Community knowledge holders emphasize its importance across generations.

3. **Contemporary Relevance**: Modern scholarship and community efforts continue to explore and preserve the middle passage and gullah geechee ancestry, ensuring it remains a living tradition.
