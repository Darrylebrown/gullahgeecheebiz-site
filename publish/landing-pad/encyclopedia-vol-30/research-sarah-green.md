## Research Topic: The Gullah Geechee diaspora and modern migration patterns

### Key Findings

1. **Historical Context**: The Gullah Geechee people have preserved African traditions in the Sea Islands for over 400 years. The Gullah Geechee diaspora and modern migration patterns represents a vital thread in this cultural tapestry.

2. **Cultural Significance**: This topic is central to understanding Gullah Geechee identity and continuity. Community knowledge holders emphasize its importance across generations.

3. **Contemporary Relevance**: Modern scholarship and community efforts continue to explore and preserve the gullah geechee diaspora and modern migration patterns, ensuring it remains a living tradition.
